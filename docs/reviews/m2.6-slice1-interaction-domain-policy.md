## Lifecycle correction and authority ownership

The bounded review found one root contract gap in the initial candidate:
`INTERRUPTED` was grouped with non-closed Sessions even though ADR 0039 defines
it as immutable terminal, and Session/Operation codecs validated individual
records without defining their legal transitions. Leaving that gap for Slice 2
would have forced the Store to invent lifecycle semantics or duplicate them
inside persistence code.

The correction keeps one owner:

- the Domain defines terminal Session types and the exact legal Session and
  Operation transition matrices;
- the later Store may only enforce those Domain transitions atomically with
  audit, uniqueness, replay, reopen, and compare-and-set behavior; and
- the later Coordinator may request transitions but cannot redefine them.

Terminal Sessions and Operations cannot transition. Session authority and
configuration are immutable, versions advance exactly once, causal time cannot
move backward, normal close passes through `CLOSING`, retention exhaustion has
its one direct-close path, and Focus may change only during an `OPEN -> OPEN`
update. Operations may move only from `RESERVED` to one terminal outcome while
preserving the complete reservation authority. The fix introduces no
compatibility alias, fallback path, or second source of lifecycle truth.
