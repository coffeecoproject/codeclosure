and dependency map required by the Slice 1 review. It assigns three exact pre-
existing contract gaps to their owning layers and batches, then sequences Store
authority, focused proof, bounded review, and commits before Slice 3 may begin.

plan, proof-owner map, or completion review. It MUST NOT reinterpret an
Interaction record, authorize a Route or action, or weaken the governing plans
and accepted ADRs. A conflict stops implementation and the higher-authority
record controls.

M2.6, or the product.

## 3. Fixed ownership and transaction map

The Domain owns closed record validity, and deterministic Runtime policies own
trusted semantic decisions. Runtime authors trusted Route Decisions, Focus,
Pending Actions, Resolutions, Reservations, handoffs, Answers, and admitted
result inputs. It also owns one exhaustive public-outcome mapper over strictly
decoded retained command-outcome types; that mapper is the sole producer of the
closed Interaction disposition and safe action-projection input. Domain cannot
depend on Runtime result types, and Store code cannot duplicate that policy.
The existing public Intake or Goal command Store remains the sole author of its
command outcome. The Interaction Store resolves that exact retained outcome,
invokes the one Runtime mapper, and atomically derives and persists one
immutable `InteractionActionOutcome` envelope from the exact retained
Reservation and mapper output. The Interaction Store also owns exact decode,
atomic persistence, CAS, uniqueness, replay/conflict classification, audit
membership, immediate read, and strict reopen. A Proposal, Assistant output,
caller-authored outcome, classification or projection, displayed message, or
audit row cannot become a competing semantic authority.

There MUST NOT be a public generic `completeInteractionOperation` Store port.
Each successful Operation completes only inside the transaction that also
persists or binds its exact result owner. Shared private decode, audit, update,
and reopen helpers MAY be reused when they preserve that rule.

The existing F3 reservation port remains available only for Operation shapes
that require no companion authority record at reservation time. Its public type
and Store boundary MUST reject a new Assistant-bound `ROUTE` reservation and a
new `INTAKE_CLARIFICATION` reservation. B1's Manifest-plus-Route transaction
and B4's clarification-Handoff-plus-Operation transaction are their respective
sole creation paths. Exact replay and failure/interruption terminalization of a
legally retained Operation continue through the existing meanings; no second
terminalization owner is introduced.

## 4. Execution strategy

The prerequisite and remaining batches are serial review boundaries:

`P0 -> B1 -> B2 -> B3 -> B4 -> B5 -> Slice 3`

A later batch MUST NOT activate behavior that depends on an earlier batch until
the earlier batch has passed its focused checks, bounded findings-first review,
and commit. Parallel test execution is allowed inside a batch, but authority
changes remain serial.

P0 and each batch follow this loop:

1. begin from a clean tree and confirm the owning records and transaction;
2. implement the smallest coherent owning change; B1 through B5 use a result-
   specific Store path and P0 remains Domain-only;
3. add rollback, replay/conflict, concurrency, immediate-read, and strict-
   reopen proof directly affected by that path;
4. run focused checks and the complete quality gate when the change crosses
   multiple authority records;
5. perform one bounded findings-first review and one confirmation review after
   material fixes; and
6. commit before beginning the next batch.

Repeated review without a changed diff, failed check, new risk, or unresolved
finding SHOULD NOT delay the next batch.

## 5. Batch plan

### P0 — Pre-B1 contract alignment

P0 is a prerequisite correction, not a Slice 2 persistence batch and not a
reopening of the historical Slice 1 verdict.

#### Scope

- Add one closed retained `FrontstageContextManifest` Domain record, owning
  codec, canonical projection, digest profile, and relationship invariants.
  It binds the preallocated Route Operation ID and reservation version, not the
  Operation digest; its compiler and Assistant Adapter remain Slice 3 work.
- Refine `InteractionMessageHandoff` into one explicitly discriminated closed
  union with an action-authorized Intake member and one distinct clarification
  member. The clarification member binds the exact Session, retained user
  Message, current Intake-Question Focus and Question digests, preallocated
  M2.5 Command ID, canonical command input digest, and causal time without a
  Pending Action.
- Preserve the action-authorized member's existing required fields and meaning;
  type `InteractionActionChain.handoff` to that member only. The clarification
  member cannot carry Pending Action, Resolution, or Action Reservation fields
  and cannot authorize a capability. Do not combine the variants into one
  optional-field bag or infer the member from field presence.
- Update the directly affected Domain fixtures, frozen operation/result
  relationships, and current-status documentation, then issue a bounded
  correction review before B1.

#### Boundary

- Do not add SQLite persistence, an Assistant compiler/Adapter, Coordinator,
  public M2.5 invocation, CLI behavior, or recovery execution.
- Do not reinterpret the accepted rule that clarification has no Pending
  Action, or let the new record become authorization.
- Do not add a second clarification Question, Answer, Answer Binding, or
  clarification-chain authority; the new member remains exact one-message
  provenance for the existing M2.5 clarification command.
- Do not edit the historical Slice 1 review to claim the gap never existed.
- If the closed record shapes require changing an accepted architectural
  decision rather than implementing it, stop for an ADR instead of resolving
  the conflict in this plan.

#### Exit gate

- The Manifest and each discriminated Handoff member have one exact semantic
  owner, strict unknown-field codecs, canonical digest vectors, size limits,
  and cross-record substitution tests.
- The action-authorized member is the only legal Handoff in
  `InteractionActionChain`; the clarification member, a mixed-member object,
  and an unknown discriminant fail at the owning type/codec/invariant boundary.
- The Domain can represent one clarification command identity before its later
  public call without a Pending Action or second Question/Answer authority;
  B4 remains its sole persistence owner.
- An Assistant-bound Route reservation cannot name a missing or mismatched
  Context Manifest once B1 supplies persistence.
- The bounded correction review passes and the tree is committed before B1.

### B1 — Route result authority

#### Scope

- Keep the proposal-free result path over an already reserved `ROUTE`
  Operation. Add one Assistant-bound reservation path that atomically persists
  the P0-defined Context Manifest with its reserved `ROUTE` Operation before
  any later Assistant call.
- Narrow the existing generic F3 reservation port at its public type and Store
  boundary so it cannot create an Assistant-bound `ROUTE`; the new atomic
  Manifest-plus-Route path is the sole creator of that shape. Do not rely on a
  caller convention or a later strict-reopen check to close this boundary.
- Atomically persist an optional Route Proposal, one Runtime-authored Route
  Decision, the exact `ROUTE_DECIDED` completed Operation, and their audits.
- Support both deterministic routes with no Proposal and Assistant-proposed
  routes whose Proposal remains untrusted input.
- Validate exact Session, retained user Message, optional current Focus,
  routing-policy, Context Manifest, Assistant Profile/Adapter/response
  contract, Operation, Proposal, Decision, and result references using the
  existing Domain meanings without re-running policy inside SQLite.
- Add typed applied/replayed/not-found/version/conflict results, immediate
  reads, strict reopen, and any additive SQLite backstop required by the exact
  relationship.

#### Boundary

- Do not call an Assistant or add the Slice 3 compiler/Adapter packages.
- Do not create a Pending Action, Frontstage Answer, output message, Goal view,
  Intake handoff, or public command outcome.
- Do not let Proposal content or identifiers author the Route Decision.
- Do not add a generic successful Operation completion port.

#### Exit gate

- Direct deterministic Route completion succeeds with no Proposal.
- A fixture Proposal may be retained only when its exact persisted Manifest,
  reserved Operation, and Runtime Decision bind it; fixture-only identity is
  not a substitute for the retained parent record.
- Cross-Session, stale Focus/session, substituted Proposal/Decision/result,
  duplicate identity, and missing relationship fail closed.
- Failure injection at every compound-write boundary rolls back all Route
  authority; concurrent consumers produce one winner and a typed loser.
- Exact replay returns retained authority without creating another record or
  implying another Assistant call.
- A new Assistant-bound Route cannot be created through the generic F3
  reservation port, while legal retained replay and failure/interruption
  closure preserve their existing meanings.
- Strict reopen accepts both legal Route shapes and rejects partial or tampered
  ones while historical failed/interrupted Operations retain their meaning.

### B2 — Pending Action, Resolution, and Reservation authority

#### Scope

- Persist a Pending Action only from one exact retained Route Decision and
  originating user Message.
- When deterministic policy selects direct authorization, commit the Pending
  Action, its exact direct Resolution, one Action Reservation, the
  `ACTION_PROPOSAL` Operation completion, and audits atomically.
- For separate confirmation, retain the unresolved Pending Action first and
  later resolve it only against the exact retained response Message.
- For a confirmed response, commit its authorized Resolution, one Action
  Reservation, the `ACTION_CONFIRMATION` Operation completion, and audits
  atomically. A non-authorization Resolution completes the same Operation
  without a Reservation.
- Enforce at most one unresolved current Pending Action per Session from the
  absence of a Resolution, using one serialized Store transaction, an additive
  SQLite insertion trigger over that derived relationship, and strict-reopen
  rejection. Do not persist a mutable current/consumed flag.

#### Boundary

- Do not invoke Intake or Goal capabilities.
- Do not derive authorization from Proposal/model fields, displayed text,
  reason-trace prose, or an audit event.
- Do not add a mutable consumed flag or a second current-Pending-Action owner.
- Exact M2.5 clarification remains outside Pending Action authority.

#### Exit gate

- Direct, confirmed, declined, unclear, expired, stale, conflict, and
  interrupted dispositions obey the closed Domain matrix.
- Concurrent Pending Action creation, reused/cross-Session/stale Pending
  Actions, and competing resolutions or reservations produce one winner and
  typed losers without invoking a capability.
- No committed authorized Resolution exists without its exact Reservation.
- Atomic rollback, exact replay, immediate read, SQLite uniqueness/backstops,
  and strict reopen pass for every implemented shape.
